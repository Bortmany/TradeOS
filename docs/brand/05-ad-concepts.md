# TradeOS: 20 Ad Concepts

Brand and voice are locked: straightforward, numbers-literate, no hype. No ad promises returns, payouts or "passing". No rocket, moon or "get rich" language. Every ad ends on the card **"For educational analytics only. Not financial advice."** The CTA is always **"Start the 14-day trial, no card."** Nothing below claims a capability that is not built: CSV import from Topstep, Apex, Tradovate, NinjaTrader, Rithmic, IBKR plus the live TopstepX/ProjectX connector; the no-code rule engine; the deterministic 0 to 100 discipline score per trade and per day with rule-by-rule pass/fail; analytics; the prop-firm tracker with Topstep/Apex/TPT presets; trade replay; PDF reports.

Copy anchors used throughout: "Your trading, held to your own rules." / "The discipline engine for day traders" / "Built for funded & live day traders" / "Score-first".

## GROUP 1: PERFORMANCE (direct response, feed and Reels)

**1. A Rule You Already Had**
- Type: Performance
- Angle: Pain-point
- Hook: "You didn't lose the account on a bad setup. You lost it on a rule you already had."
- Visual: A trader sits motionless in front of a dark monitor after the close; a monospace list types in ("Max 2 losses ✗", "No trades after 11:00 ✗"). Cut to the real dashboard with the score ring and the failed rule highlighted.
- CTA: Start the 14-day trial, no card.

**2. Journal vs Engine**
- Type: Performance
- Angle: Comparison
- Hook: "A journal tells you what happened. It never tells you whether you followed your plan."
- Visual: Split screen: left, a paper journal page and a spreadsheet of P&L; right, the real TradeOS graded-trade card with Pass/Pass/Fail/Pass rows. The left side dims, the right side stays lit in blue.
- CTA: Start the 14-day trial, no card.

**3. Scored 72**
- Type: Performance (product-led)
- Angle: Curiosity
- Hook: "This trade scored 72. It lost money. Only one of those matters."
- Visual: Black frame, the number 72 types in amber; reveal of the real journal detail with the loss in red and the rule breakdown, the single Fail row pulsing once. Score ring with its four sub-scores closes the ad.
- CTA: Start the 14-day trial, no card.

**4. Wrong Column**
- Type: Performance
- Angle: "You're doing this wrong"
- Hook: "You're grading your trades by P&L. That's the wrong column."
- Visual: A spreadsheet column of green and red numbers scrolls, then a new column slides in beside it: discipline scores in monospace, several red trades with high scores and green trades with low ones. Cut to the real dashboard.
- CTA: Start the 14-day trial, no card.

**5. Least Useful Number**
- Type: Performance
- Angle: Contrarian
- Hook: "Your P&L is the least useful number on your screen."
- Visual: A monitor with only a big P&L figure; it shrinks into a small secondary tile as the real TradeOS score ring takes the top-left of the frame (the score-first layout). Text: "Lead with the number you can actually control."
- CTA: Start the 14-day trial, no card.

## SELECTED FOR PRODUCTION

**Concept A: Sticky Note (No. 6, UGC-style)**
One trader, one home desk, one physical prop that every day trader recognizes. It is the cheapest ad to make and the easiest to believe, and it sets up the rule-row Pass/Fail screen as the natural payoff.

**Concept B: Scored 72 (No. 3, product-led)**
The score screen and a graded trade with rule-by-rule Pass/Fail are the entire ad; nothing to invent, nothing to act. It states the product's one real difference (process scored, not outcome) in a single number.

**Concept C: Buffer Remaining (No. 14, cinematic)**
The prop-firm tracker is the category-winning surface from the research and the sharpest fear in the audience. Cinematic treatment gives the brand its one "big" ad without a single hype word.

**Concept D: Five Minutes (No. 16, product demo)**
Answers the biggest trust complaint in this category (paying before seeing the product) with the free, no-card trial on the trader's own data. Doubles as the landing-page embed.

**Concept E: A Rule You Already Had (No. 1, performance)**
The most direct pain-point hook, written the way traders actually talk about a blown account. Strong for cold Meta and TikTok traffic and pairs with Concept A for retargeting.
