# TradeOS: Production Ad Packages (Concepts A to E)

Five 30-second vertical ads, ready for Higgsfield (Kling 3.0) generation and edit. Every ad ends on the same card: **"For educational analytics only. Not financial advice."** No ad promises profit, returns, payouts or "passing" a challenge. The product is the discipline score, the rulebook, and the rule-by-rule grade.

Global rules for every prompt below:

- Format: 9:16, 1080x1920, 10 seconds per scene, photoreal, commercial quality, dark moody desk lighting, one electric blue accent light (#5593f7). No other colored light.
- Any screen that shows the product must use the real TradeOS dashboard: **the monitor/phone shows the real TradeOS dashboard (real screenshot composited in post); do not invent UI.** Generate the screen as a dark, mostly blank glowing panel and composite `public/screenshots/dashboard-dark.png` or `journal-dark.png` in the edit.
- Green (#2dbe7a) and red (#e64c57) appear only inside the product UI where they mean P&L or pass/fail. Never as set lighting.
- Text on screen: system sans, numerals in monospace, off-white #eaedf0 on #0a0b0f. Score numbers in blue when neutral, amber #f6b128 when mid (60 to 79).
- Voice: calm, male or female, mid-30s, no hype, no shouting. Music: low analog synth pulse, 80 to 90 bpm, no drops.

## CONCEPT A: "Sticky Note"
